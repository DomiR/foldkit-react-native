Add experimental strict stacks, tabs, and declarative Tap, LongPress, Pan, and Swipe gestures. Navigation is rendered from the Model, host navigation actions become Messages, and gesture callbacks carry portable pan translation and velocity values. The web adapter supplies stack headers, tab bars, and lifecycle-scoped pointer handling. Screen and tab Titles label stack headers and tab buttons without adding HTML title attributes to their content.
